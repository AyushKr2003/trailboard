# Welcome to Trailboard

Trailboard is a browser without tabs. Every page is a **live card** on one large board that you can pan and zoom. When you open a link, the new page lands **beside** the page it came from, joined by a line, so every search leaves a **trail** you can see, rearrange and come back to.

> Lost at any point? Press `Esc` to get back to the board, `Shift+F` to see everything, `Ctrl+K` to find anything, and `?` for every shortcut.

---

## First steps

1. Press `Ctrl+T`, type an address or a search, and press `Enter`.
2. Click a link in that page. It opens as a new card beside the first one.
3. Press `Esc` to step back to the board, then `Tab` to read the current card full-window. `Tab` again returns.
4. Drag the board to pan, and use `Ctrl` + scroll to zoom.

## Where your keys go

This is the one thing to learn first. The keyboard talks either to the **board** or to a **page**:

- **A thin ring** around a card means it is the *current* card. Keys act on the board: `h j k l` or the arrow keys move between cards, and single letters are commands.
- **A thick ring** means the page *has the keys*. You are typing into the website, just like in any browser.
- Click inside a page, or press `Enter`, to give it the keys. Press `Esc` to take them back.

## Moving around

- **Pan:** drag empty board, middle-drag, or scroll over the board
- **Zoom:** `Ctrl` + scroll, or `+` and `-`
- **Next card:** `h j k l` or the arrow keys
- **See everything:** `Shift+F` · **Fill the view with a card:** `z` or double-click its title bar
- **Read full-window:** `Tab` (`Esc` returns)
- **Move or resize a card:** drag its title bar or its bottom-right corner

## Opening and finding pages

- **New card:** `Ctrl+T` · **Change the current page's address:** `Ctrl+L`
- **Find anything:** `Ctrl+K` searches open cards (even by words on the page), closed cards, history, spaces and commands
- **Open beside the current card:** `Shift+Enter` in `Ctrl+K`, `Ctrl+H` or the address bar
- **Follow a link without the mouse:** `f`, then type the label that appears
- **Back / forward / reload:** `Alt+←` / `Alt+→` / `Ctrl+R`
- **Find in the page:** `Ctrl+F` · **Page zoom:** `Ctrl++` / `Ctrl+-` / `Ctrl+0` (remembered per site)
- **Close / reopen:** `Ctrl+W` / `Ctrl+Shift+T` · **Undo a board change:** `Ctrl+Z`

## Keeping research in order

- **Trails sidebar:** `B` lists every trail as a tree. Rest the pointer on the window's left edge to peek at it.
- **Tidy:** `T` lays every trail out left to right, with nothing overlapping
- **Fold a trail into a stack:** `S` (again to unfold) · **Flip through a stack:** `[` and `]`
- **Name or colour a trail:** `R` / `C`, or click the trail's tag above its first card
- **Walk along a trail:** `Alt+PgDn` / `Alt+PgUp`
- **Select several cards:** `Shift`+drag on the board, then drag or close them together

## Notes

- **New note:** `N` · **Sticky note:** `Shift+N` (`C` changes its colour)
- Notes are Markdown. Click one to write, and press `Esc` to see it formatted.
- **Quote a page:** select text, right-click, then choose *Add to note*
- **Export:** `Ctrl+S` saves the current note, or the whole board with its trails, as Markdown in `Documents/Trailboard`

## Spaces

A space is a separate board, for a separate project.

- **New space:** `Ctrl+Shift+N` · **Switch:** `Ctrl+PgDn` / `Ctrl+PgUp` · **Rename:** `F2`
- **Private space:** `Ctrl+Shift+P`. Its pages stay out of history, and its cookies are forgotten when you quit.

## Good to know

- **Dimmed cards are asleep.** To save memory, only the few cards you use most stay live; the rest show their last picture. Click one, or bring it into view, to wake it. Change how many stay awake in the settings.
- **Everything is saved as you go.** Your boards, spaces and notes come back exactly as you left them on the next start.
- **Ads, trackers and cookie banners are blocked.** The shield on a card's title bar shows how many. Click it to allow ads on a site that needs them.
- **History** (`Ctrl+H`) remembers the pages you opened and their text, so `Ctrl+K` finds them later. `Shift+Delete` forgets a page, and *Keep history* in the settings turns it off.
- **Downloads** go straight to your Downloads folder. `Ctrl+J` lists them.
- **Settings:** `Ctrl+,` covers the search engine, scroll speed, page colours, history and ad blocking.

---

**`?` shows every shortcut at any time.** This guide is an ordinary note: edit it, keep it, or close it with `Ctrl+W`. You can bring it back with *Welcome note* in `Ctrl+K`.
