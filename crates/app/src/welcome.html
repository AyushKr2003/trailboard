Welcome to Trailboard

Browse on a board, not in tabs.

Every page is a live card on one infinite board. Open a link and it lands beside the page it came from, joined by a line, so every search leaves a trail you can see, rearrange and pick up again tomorrow.

Three ideas

Everything else follows from these.

Cards

Each page is a live card. Move it, resize it, zoom out to see them all. Pages keep running, so nothing reloads when you look away.

Trails

Links open beside their source and stay joined. A trail is the path of one line of thought: name it, colour it, fold it into a stack.

Spaces

A space is a whole separate board, one per project. Switch between them in a keystroke; each remembers exactly where you were.

Your first minute

Four steps, and you know the basics.

Open a page

Press CtrlT, type an address or a search, then Enter.

Follow a link

Click any link. The new page appears beside the old one, joined by a trail.

Step back

Press Esc for the board. Drag to pan, Ctrl + scroll to zoom.

Focus

Press Tab to read the current card full-window, and Tab again to return.

Where your keys go

The one thing worth learning first.

On the board · thin ring

  • The card is current, and keys drive the board.
  • hjkl or the arrow keys move between cards.
  • Single letters are commands: B trails, T tidy, N note.
Enter or click →
← Esc

In the page · heavy ring

  • The page has the keys, just like any browser tab.
  • Type into forms, scroll, use the site's own shortcuts.
  • Esc hands the keys back to the board.

Good to know

So nothing comes as a surprise.

Dimmed cards are asleep

To save memory only the cards you use most stay live. The rest show their last picture until you click them or bring them into view.

Saved as you go

Boards, spaces and notes are saved continuously and come back exactly as you left them. There is nothing to save by hand.

Ads and trackers blocked

Ads, trackers and cookie banners are stopped. The shield on a card's title bar counts them; click it to allow a site that needs ads.

Search remembers words

History keeps the text of pages you open, so CtrlK finds them by any word. CtrlH lists them; turn it off in Settings.

Private spaces

CtrlShiftP makes a space private: its pages stay out of history and its cookies are forgotten when you quit.

Downloads, no dialogs

Files go straight to your Downloads folder. CtrlJ lists them, and Ctrl, opens Settings for the rest.
