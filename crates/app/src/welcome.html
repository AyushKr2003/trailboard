The one thing to learn first

Where your keys go.

A thin ring means the board has the keys; a heavy ring means the page does. Click the card to swap.

Board has the keysPage has the keys
  • hjkl or the arrows move between cards.
  • Single letters are commands: T tidies, N adds a note.
  • Enter or a click hands the keys to the page.
  • The page has the keys, just like any browser tab.
  • Type into forms, scroll, use the site's own shortcuts.
  • Esc hands the keys back to the board.

Good to know

So nothing comes as a surprise.

Dimmed cards are asleep

Only the cards you use most stay live, to save memory. The rest show their last picture until you click them or bring them into view.

Saved as you go

Boards, spaces and notes are saved all the time and come back exactly as you left them. There is nothing to save by hand.

Ads and trackers blocked

Ads, trackers and cookie banners are stopped. The shield on a card's label counts them; click it to allow a site that needs ads.

Search remembers words

History keeps the text of the pages you open, so CtrlK finds them by any word. CtrlH lists them; turn it off in Settings.

Private spaces

CtrlShiftP makes a space private: its pages stay out of history and its cookies are forgotten when you quit.

Downloads, no dialogs

Files go straight to your Downloads folder. CtrlJ lists them, and Ctrl, opens Settings for the rest.
